## 🥃 The Art of Tasting, Elevated

Every dram tells a centuries-old story of peat, copper, toasted oak, and time. **Aqua Vitaeum** transforms the ephemeral experience of fine spirits into a permanent, beautifully visual sensory ledger.

Designed with the quiet elegance of a private tasting room, it bridges timeless craftsmanship with modern sensory science.

┌────────────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────┐

### 1. 📖 The Archival Tasting Journal
Record every nuance with master distiller precision:
- **Distillery & Region Ledger**: Islay smoke, Speyside orchard fruits, Kentucky rye spice, or Japanese mizunara oak.
- **Cask Maturation Specs**: First-fill Oloroso sherry, virgin American oak, port pipes, or peated quarter casks.
- **Precious Metal Scoring**: Automatic noble metal tier classification from **Radiant 24k Gold (≥90)** to **Toasted Copper (70–79)**.

### 2. 🕸️ Dual-Layer Sensory Radar Profiling
Witness the evolution between the glass and the palate:
- Real-time 11-axis radar chart mapping **Nose Aromas** (*Warm Amber Layer*) directly over **Palate Flavors** (*Clover Green Layer*).
- Instantly reveal hidden complexities — discover when a dram's aroma promises honey and heather, but delivers volcanic peat and sea salt on the tongue.

### 3. ⏱️ 60-Second Finish Time-Intensity Spline
The true test of an exceptional spirit lies in the finish:
- Interactive cubic Bezier spline modeling the **temporal intensity** of the finish from first sip to extinction.
- Track immediate attack, plateau duration, and lingering oak warmth over a full 60-second temporal horizon.

### 4. 🏛️ The Grand Cellar Vault & Journals
Organize your collection with seamless layout fluidity:
- **Artisanal Photo Cards**: High-resolution gallery presentation with tasting tags and region badges.
- **Compact Archival List**: Fast scanning for extensive cellars and quick dram selection.
- **Sortable Matrix Table**: Deep analytics overview in signature Irish Clover Green styling.
- **Curated Journals**: Partition your collection into thematic volumes (e.g. *Islay Peat Explorations*, *Cask Strength Bourbons*, *Private Bottlings*).

## 🎨 Tactile Aesthetics & Craftsmanship

Aqua Vitaeum is meticulously engineered around the atmosphere of an archival tasting room:

- 📜 **Warm Vintage Linen Canvas (`#ECE5D8`)**: A natural, unhurried, glare-free background with tactile paper warmth.
- 🪶 **Letterpress Walnut Ink (`#2B1E14`)**: Highest-contrast typography achieving certified **13.8:1 WCAG AAA** readability.
- 🍀 **Irish Clover Green Accents (`#2E945D`)**: Fresh botanical highland tones for badges, active triggers, and table headers.
- 🕯️ **Optional Vintage Dark Pub Mode**: Switch seamlessly into a warm, candlelit tasting lounge for evening sessions.
- 📶 **Cellar-Ready Offline PWA**: Works fully offline deep in stone barrel cellars or remote Scottish distilleries without an internet connection.

## 🚀 Experience Aqua Vitaeum

### Quickstart

```bash
# Clone the codex
git clone https://github.com/moujou/aquavitaeum.git
cd aquavitaeum

# Install dependencies
npm install

# Launch your tasting atelier
npm run dev
```

Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser to begin recording your tasting notes.
